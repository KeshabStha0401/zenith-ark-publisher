Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

ZENITH ARK Publisher ("the app") is a personal publishing tool operated by the owner of the ZENITH ARK channels for use with their own accounts only.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Drive (drive.file scope): only files the app itself creates — the owner's generated videos and images.
  • YouTube: uploading videos to the owner's channel and reading its basic channel details.
  • TikTok, Instagram, Facebook: publishing content to the owner's own accounts and Pages.

How data is used

Access is used solely to upload and store the owner's own content. No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer with restricted file permissions and are never transmitted anywhere except to the platform that issued them.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from each platform's connected-apps settings (for Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions), after which the local tokens are deleted.
